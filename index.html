TVmate privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

TVmate is a TV player for Android TV made by Brent Rosen. This page explains what information the app handles. In short: TVmate has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer receives none of your information.

What stays on your TV

Your playlists, programme guides, logins, favourites, watch history, recordings list, profiles and settings are stored on your TV only. The developer has no server and never receives them.

Services the app connects to

To work, TVmate connects directly from your TV to these services:

  • Sources you add yourself: your playlist provider, programme guide addresses, your Plex account and Plex servers, and your own network storage. TVmate sends them only what they need to sign you in and deliver your content, such as the username and password you entered for them.
  • Plex sign-in: you sign in on plex.tv with a code; TVmate never sees your Plex password.
  • Public information services: The Movie Database (titles and artwork), ESPN (sports scores and schedules), Wikipedia (channel logos) and internet radio directories and stations. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any internet connection reveals, such as your IP address.
  • Approximate location for local radio: to suggest radio stations near you, the Radio section looks up your city from your internet (IP) address using ipapi.co, or ipwho.is if that fails. It sends the resulting approximate position to the Radio Browser directory to find nearby stations. The location is kept on your TV and is not used for anything else.
  • Voice search: when you use the microphone, speech is turned into text by your TV's speech service (Google). TVmate receives only the text.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Backups

A backup contains a profile's settings and logins. TVmate writes it only to places you choose: the TV itself or your own network storage. Treat backup files as private.

Children

TVmate is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Everything TVmate stores is on your TV. Deleting a profile, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it. Backups you saved on your own storage can be deleted there.
