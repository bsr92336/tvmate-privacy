TVmate privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

TVmate is an independent Android TV player made by Brent Rosen for family and friends. It has no developer-operated analytics or advertising SDK. Streaming providers may include advertising in their content. TVmate is not affiliated with TiviMate or the services described below.

What stays on your TV

TVmate stores profiles, playlists, programme guides, provider credentials, favourites, viewing progress and settings in its app storage. It uses these to play and organize the sources you choose. Some information also goes to your selected services, other household devices or storage as described below. In version 0.2.1 and later, Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled by the app; older sideload versions may allow Android backup. Manual profile backups remain available.

Services the app connects to

To work, TVmate connects directly from your TV to these services:

  • Sources you add yourself: your playlist provider, programme guide addresses, your Plex account and Plex servers, and your own network storage. TVmate sends them only what they need to sign you in and deliver your content, such as the username and password you entered for them.
  • Plex: you sign in on plex.tv with a code; TVmate receives an access token, not your Plex password. TVmate requests your libraries and sends playback progress and watched/unwatched actions to your selected Plex server to keep watch state in sync.
  • Titles, artwork and sports: TVmate requests titles, identifiers, artwork, logos, scores and schedules from The Movie Database, fanart.tv, ESPN, TheSportsDB, Wikipedia/Wikimedia and the sources you choose. Requests can reveal the title, team, channel or artwork being requested, plus your IP address and basic connection information. Requests can occur during background library/guide refresh and Sports Hub prefetch.
  • Approximate location for local radio: in the updated internal-test build, this is optional and off by default. If you enable “Use approximate location for radio” in Settings → Radio, TVmate looks up your city from your IP address using ipapi.co, or ipwho.is if that fails. It caches that result on the TV, normally refreshing after one week or when you request a refresh. Approximate coordinates or country are sent to Radio Browser and used to select local radio and programme information. Turning the setting off stops new lookups and clears the cached city when radio settings are observed. This is IP-based location, not GPS. Older sideloaded versions performed this lookup automatically.
  • Radio: radio directories, stations and their metadata services receive requests for stations you browse or play. Radio Browser also receives the station identifier when you play a station from its directory, for its play counts. These services receive your IP address. Their retention and use of connection and playback records are governed by their own policies.
  • Voice search: when you use the microphone, speech is turned into text by your TV's speech service (Google). TVmate receives only the text.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Recordings, shared storage and the TV home screen

If you configure network storage or a home recorder, recording requests, programme details, recordings and associated metadata are written to that storage and read by authorized household devices. The storage operator and anyone with access to those folders may see them. Some family installations use storage administered by Brent Rosen; that administrator can access data deliberately saved there. TVmate can also publish recent channels and viewing-progress recommendations to Android TV's home-screen provider.

Connections and security

Public information requests use HTTPS where supported. User-selected playlists, streams, Plex servers and network shares may use HTTP or other connections without transport encryption. TVmate therefore does not promise that all data is encrypted in transit. Use trusted sources and encrypted connections where available. Credentials and profile backup files are sensitive; protect your TV and storage access.

Backups

A manual backup contains a profile's settings and logins and may include viewing history and provider data. It is written to the TV or the network location you configure. Backups may also be scheduled by your settings. Anyone with the file may be able to access its contents; do not share it publicly.

Children

TVmate is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Local profile data remains until you remove the profile or clear the app's storage; caches may be replaced during normal use. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-private local data. It does not delete recordings or backups on shared storage, Plex watch state, or records held by external services. Delete those through the relevant storage or service. TVmate does not create a developer-hosted user account. Contact Brent Rosen below for help with data on storage he administers; third-party account deletion and retention follow the relevant provider's policies.
